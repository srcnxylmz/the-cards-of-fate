## Shared fragments → molds (memory)

Beginning: flint_fragments offers carrying a common repair stock at a real supply cost versus trading it for immediate food and losing some repair capacity. The due repair_right waits for the village era; it is not injected by a later scene. Branch: shared_repair_stock unlocks potter_lease.commons; barter does not. A general paid kiln trial remains a lawful alternative. Discovering smelting enables copper_recast, where actual common mold production is available only with the earlier stock.

Conclusion: mold_audit requires the delayed repair right and a completed recasting decision. It distinguishes paying for new labor under open access (charter), taking immediate private-lease income (lease), or negotiating from actual common production (partners). The last option requires shared_recasting_tools. Public access does not mean confiscating every new mold for free; private access has later social/supply costs. Each outcome carries a delayed consequence, resolved_mold_audit and a public/private closure flag. The event cannot recur after closure, while ordinary recasting remains a recurring production problem.

Completion for the reported natural shared path requires ordered actual decisions stock → commons → common → partners/charter. The private path requires barter → trial/repairers → repair/trial → lease/charter. Merely possessing one flag or selecting the final card does not count. Controlled engine regressions traverse both opposite branches, the selector, real era-delayed queues, save/reload and terminal exclusions. Natural completion counters are emitted by CrisisDiagnostics and reported separately per policy.

TR/EN review: each version identifies who carried the original burden, who performs new work and who receives access. Neither translation treats past communal work as ownership of all future labor. The private branch is not phrased as an unexplained resource reward. The new hearing closes the access dispute, not every future economic disagreement.

## Fish passage → city water account (river_memory)

Beginning: salmon_weir.open sacrifices today's catch to leave a passage; close does not unlock reed_bank. Branch: preserving the reed buffer grants reed_filtration, while building on it has different costs and no inherited filtering practice. Both queues wait until the city era before reed_water_memory appears. Roof cistern work must actually be completed before cistern_account; the earlier flag alone cannot skip that work.

Conclusion: the audit recognizes old land contribution and current labor together; current counts only today's work and has different results on a worn/sound bank. inherited requires the actual roof-cistern adaptation of the protected reeds. Public/private outcomes include paid oversight/hauling consequences and resolved_cistern_account, ending this particular accounting dispute. Roof maintenance remains recurring and is not a free permanent water reward.

Reported natural preserved completion requires ordered open → buffer → reeds → inherited/audit. Built-bank completion requires open → build → filter → current/audit. Controlled engine tests prove the contrary initial choice blocks the branch, the city queue waits, roof history cannot be skipped, both endings are reachable and closed, and saves preserve the decision-specific availability.

TR/EN review: the earlier bank contribution and the new upkeep obligation remain distinct, and neither is treated as an unlimited exemption. The private ruling protects short-term accounting at a stated political cost; environmental context changes its human outcome. No card repeats the old writing-law dispute or claims that clearing a cistern alone resolves all city water rights.
