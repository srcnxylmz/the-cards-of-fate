# 90-card study inputs

All seed-policy runs use seeds 0..<10000 and a 500-decision cap. The random, survival, knowledge and progression score implementations are unchanged from 56aad88. Tests/fixtures isolate story predicates and are not proofs of every natural path in one playthrough.

| Directory | Meaning |
|---|---|

| milestone90-76-before | Exact original 76 deck; progression rerun with new post-smelting observations, before gameplay changes; original 9879/7034 era reach reproduced |
| milestone90-76-intermediate | First repair: granary/irrigation, paid sanitation and history-aware choices; before separating cultivation from storage; 7554 Bronze |
| milestone90-76-repair | Frozen repaired 76-card deck with final production rules; four policies; 9681 progression Bronze. Dry/cache remain their first authored low-yield versions, so comparison to final 90 is a combined change, not pure content isolation |

| milestone90-pre-food | Final production and early harvest returns, but the first dry/cache supplies; four policies; survival mean 68.641 and progression Bronze 9557 |
| milestone90-after | Final 90 (30/30/30), 203 decisions, final production, dry/cache supplies 12 with ecological/spoilage costs; four final policy reports |

The calendar/early-return-only trial reached 6520 Bronze before field production was repaired. This measured result is documented but its complete intermediate JSON was superseded locally; do not pretend that raw trial is available here.

Final survival JSON was reused from the separately executed food study only after byte equality of the experimental and canonical 90-card decks. The other three final policies were rerun afterward. Logs remain local under the study/artifact directories; raw JSON and necessary frozen decks are committed explicitly. These decks are diagnostic inputs, never runtime resources.

Post-metal stage losses exclude the discovery decision itself and include later era-2 decisions while owning smelting. Acquisition means condition on acquiring the technology. Final era-2 failure gates overlap; exposure-normalized tables distinguish shorter time before transition from loss frequency. The old preTransition ending field precedes terminal evaluation and is not used as a blocker measure. Actual ending distributions and final-state blockers are measured after real resolution.

Capped civilizations are living censored runs. No report certifies 45–90-minute human pacing, absence of every optimal resource loop, approved historical art, physical-device quality or complete story-chain editorial acceptance.
